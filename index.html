About Carolina

Carolina Barreiro Valdez is a transformation and Change Management leader focused on educational technology and social impact across Latin America

She holds an MBA with distinction in Agile Leadership and Transformation and brings 7+ years of experience partnering with senior stakeholders to turn evidence into practical strategy, governance, and operating model change. Her research explores how Agile becomes lived culture in Mexican EdTech under real constraints, and her social impact work centres gender equity in STEM through her leadership of Beautiful Patterns, an MIT affiliated initiative. She is motivated by responsible AI, measurable outcomes, and institutions that want to transform with ethical clarity.

Led agile transformation initiatives and managed cross-functional IT teams in implementing Scrum methodologies and digital transformation strategies.
2020 - 2021
Project Mgmt & Digital Transformation Specialist
After falling in love with Project Management through my 2019 startup experience, I became a self-taught learner. Awarded scholarships for MIT Professional Education's Leading Digital Transformation and Cloud & DevOps Certificates. In 2020, I headed the PM area in a startup and pioneered a migration to agile methodologies—a significant challenge that deepened my expertise.
2018 - Present
EdTech Journey
My passion for teaching was ignited through Beautiful Patterns, a computer bootcamp for girls where I volunteered. Since 2018, I've been dedicated to sharing knowledge. I'm an instructor for the Online MERN Fullstack Certificate at Emeritus (MITxPro), web development teacher at Panamerican University, SCRUM Fundamentals instructor, and currently Director of the open-source Beautiful Patterns project.
2016 - 2020
Digital Animation Engineer
Passionate about blending Art and Engineering, I studied at Panamerican University in Mexico City with a research stay at MIT and studies abroad at DePaul University in Chicago. Graduated with honors and leveraged my university experience for projects spanning educational innovation, gender equity, project management, and art & technology.
