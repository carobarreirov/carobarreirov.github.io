About Carolina

Carolina Barreiro Valdez is a transformation and Change Management leader whose work brings together technology, education, leadership, and organisational development. With an engineering background and an MBA with distinction in Agile Leadership and Transformation, she brings an unusually interdisciplinary perspective to complex change.
Her career spans consulting, tech project management, research, and teaching across Latin America and Europe, with experience leading organisational transformation, capability building, governance design, and strategic technology adoption in corporate, educational, and innovation contexts.
Alongside her consulting work, Carolina has taught and facilitated learning experiences for professionals and students in technology and leadership environments, helping people navigate complexity with clarity and confidence. She is also the Managing Director of Beautiful Patterns, an MIT linked initiative focused on creating pathways for women in STEM. Her work is defined by analytical depth, strategic thinking, and a human centred approach to inclusive and meaningful transformation.

After falling in love with Project Management through my 2019 startup experience, I became a self-taught learner. Awarded scholarships for MIT Professional Education's Leading Digital Transformation and Cloud and DevOps Certificates. EdTech Journey

My passion for teaching was ignited through Beautiful Patterns, a computer bootcamp for girls where I volunteered. Since 2018, I've been dedicated to sharing knowledge. I'm an instructor for the Online MERN Fullstack Certificate at Emeritus (MITxPro), web development teacher at Panamerican University, SCRUM Fundamentals instructor, and currently Director of the open-source Beautiful Patterns project.

2016 - 2020

Passionate about blending Art and Engineering, I studied at Panamerican University in Mexico City with a research stay at MIT and studies abroad at DePaul University in Chicago. Graduated with honors and leveraged my university experience for projects spanning educational innovation, gender equity, project management, and art and technology.
