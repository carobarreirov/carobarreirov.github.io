Caro Barreiro

PM Specialist @Technology also EdTech enthusiast

Carolina Barreiro is a Digital Animation Engineer and Project Management Specialist from the Universidad Panamericana Mexico City. Alumni of two MIT Professional Education Certificates: Leading Digital Transformation and Cloud & DevOps. Member of the NSLS, The National Society of Leadership and Success, after her academic performance at DePaul University in Chicago.

Project Manager by profession after having worked in the area for more 3+ years. Being her expertise is agile, specifically with Scrum. She has led more than 5 teams, throughout more than 20 projects in stem areas, video games and software development.
Currently works as a Solution Architect at RedHat, the global leader in open-source enterprise technology. And, she is a course leader and instructor at Emeritus, a global EdTech company, of the MIT xPro professional certificate: “Coding: Fullstack Web Development”.
